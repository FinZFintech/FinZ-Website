Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FinZ Fintech Private Limited ("FinZ", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and discloses your personal data. It has been prepared in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), the Reserve Bank of India ("RBI") circulars on storage of payment system data, and the RBI Master Direction on Digital Lending, 2025.

1. Data We Collect

  • Identity & KYC Data: Name, date of birth, PAN, Aadhaar (masked, via Offline XML/AA), selfie, video-KYC capture.
  • Contact Data: Mobile number, email, residential address.
  • Financial Data: Bank statements, income proofs (fetched via consent on the Account Aggregator framework), credit bureau reports.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, device identifier, OS version, app version, crash logs (only when the app is in use).
  • Transactional Data: Payment history, loan servicing records.
We do NOT access: your phone contacts, media/gallery, call logs, SMS, or location, except where a specific, one-time permission is required (such as camera for video-KYC). This is in line with the RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.

2. Purpose & Legal Basis

We process personal data only for specified, lawful purposes notified to you and based on your consent, or where processing is necessary to comply with law (e.g., KYC, AML/CFT, reporting to credit bureaus and FIU-IND).

3. Storage & Data Localisation

All payment system data is stored only on servers located in India, as mandated by the RBI circular on Storage of Payment System Data (DPSS.CO.OD No.2785/06.08.005/2017-2018). Other personal data is stored in India-based data centres with replication within India.

4. Sharing of Data

We may share your data with: (i) Lending/partner banks or NBFCs; (ii) Credit Information Companies (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark); (iii) Regulators, law enforcement, or courts pursuant to a lawful request; (iv) Cloud and technology service providers bound by contractual confidentiality obligations. We do not sell your data.

5. Your Rights under the DPDP Act

  • Right to Access: Obtain a summary of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate data or erasure once the retention purpose is served.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: See our Grievance page.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal will not affect lawfulness of processing done earlier.

6. Retention

We retain personal data for the period mandated under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (generally 5 years from the end of the business relationship or transaction, whichever is later) and as required by RBI/IT Act. Data is securely deleted or anonymised thereafter.

7. Security

We deploy encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control, periodic VAPT, and SOC monitoring. Our controls are aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 and the RBI Cyber Security Framework. Cyber incidents are reported to CERT-In within 6 hours as required under the CERT-In Directions, 2022.
