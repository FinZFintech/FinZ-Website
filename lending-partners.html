Lending Partners, LSPs, TSPs & DLAs

In line with the Reserve Bank of India's Guidelines on Digital Lending (2 September 2022) and the Co-Lending Model circular, FinZ Finance Private Limited discloses below (i) the regulated lending partners with whom we have sourcing, co-lending or balance-sheet arrangements, (ii) our Lending Service Providers (LSPs), (iii) our Technology Service Providers (TSPs), and (iv) all Digital Lending Applications (DLAs). Each lending partner is itself an RBI-regulated entity; the NBFC remains fully responsible for all actions of its LSPs.

Why this matters: As a borrower, you have the right to know who ultimately lends to you, who collects repayment, which apps are authorised, and where your data flows. This page and your Key Fact Statement (KFS) name the lender and service providers relevant to your specific loan.

1. Lending Partners (Co-lending / Sourcing)

Your lending relationship

  • For every loan sanctioned, the Key Fact Statement (KFS) issued to you specifies the name of the lender (FinZ Finance Private Limited or a co-lending partner) and the share of each party's exposure, if applicable.
  • Under co-lending arrangements, both entities share customer-level credit risk in a pre-agreed ratio as per a formal co-lending agreement.
  • All servicing (EMI collection, grievances, NOC issuance) is routed through a single point of contact — FinZ Finance Private Limited — unless otherwise agreed with you in writing.

3. Technology Service Providers (TSPs)

FinZ Finance Private Limited engages Technology Service Providers (TSPs) for technology infrastructure and support. TSPs do not perform any lending activity and do not have loan-sanctioning authority. All customer-data access by TSPs is governed by strict contractual and security controls, role-based access, encryption-at-rest, and periodic VAPT.

Name of TSPNature of Technology ServiceAccess to Customer Data

4. Digital Lending Applications (DLAs)

FinZ Finance Private Limited does not own, operate, or partner with any Digital Lending Application (DLA) for loan origination, sanction, disbursement or servicing. All loan applications are sourced through direct customer interaction on our website and processed through the NBFC's internal systems.

Grievance responsibility

Regardless of which partner funds your loan or which LSP/TSP touches your application, FinZ Finance Private Limited is your first point of grievance contact. Your complaints will be acknowledged and resolved through our Grievance Redressal Mechanism. Where applicable, the complaint is simultaneously reported to the co-lending partner and the concerned LSP/TSP.

Data sharing

Personal and loan data is shared with lending partners, LSPs and TSPs strictly on a need-to-know basis for credit decisioning, KYC compliance, credit bureau reporting and servicing, with all recipients bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations. Our Privacy Policy lists every category of data shared, with whom, and for what purpose.

Note: Each of the four lists above is updated on this page within 7 days of any new engagement going live or an existing arrangement being terminated.
