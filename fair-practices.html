Fair Practices Code

Approved by the Board of Directors. Last reviewed: 15 March 2026.

This Fair Practices Code ("FPC") has been framed pursuant to the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India under the Master Direction – Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2023 and the Master Direction on Digital Lending, 2025.

1. Applications for loans and their processing

  • All communication to the borrower shall be in a language understood by the borrower.
  • Loan application forms shall include all necessary information which may affect the borrower's interest, including fees & charges, so that a meaningful comparison can be made with other lenders.
  • We shall issue an acknowledgement for receipt of every application, with the indicative timeline for disposal.

2. Loan appraisal and terms/conditions

  • A sanction letter and the Key Fact Statement (KFS) will be conveyed in writing, in English and a vernacular language as chosen by the borrower.
  • Penal interest, if charged, will be mentioned in bold in the loan agreement.
  • No fees or charges not disclosed in the KFS shall be levied at any stage.

3. Disbursement of loans including changes in terms and conditions

  • Any change in terms and conditions, including disbursement schedule, interest rates, service charges, or prepayment charges, shall be notified with prospective effect only.
  • Changes in interest rate and charges will be communicated individually, with reasonable notice.

4. General

  • We shall not interfere in the affairs of the borrower except for the purposes provided in the terms and conditions of the loan agreement.
  • In case of receipt of a request for transfer of a borrowal account, our consent or objection shall be conveyed within 21 days.
  • In recovery, we do not resort to undue harassment — no calls at odd hours, no public shaming, no coercion. Recovery agents are trained and their conduct is monitored.

5. Confidentiality

All personal information of customers shall be kept confidential and disclosed only as permitted under our Privacy Policy or mandated by law.

6. Responsible Lending Conduct (RBI Digital Lending Guidelines)

  • All loan disbursals and repayments shall be executed between the bank accounts of the borrower and the regulated entity, without any pass-through or pool account of any Lending Service Provider (LSP).
  • Any fees payable to the LSP are paid directly by FinZ, not the borrower.
  • A cooling-off / look-up period is provided in every loan contract.
  • We do not increase credit limits automatically without explicit, recorded consent of the borrower.

7. Grievance Redressal

See our Grievance Redressal page.

8. Review

This FPC and the compliance thereof shall be reviewed by the Board of Directors at least annually. A consolidated report is placed before the Board in every quarterly meeting.
