Why FinZ

We operate within the letter and spirit of the RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines (2022), the Payment & Settlement Systems Act (2007), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023).

Transparent Pricing

Every loan includes a Key Fact Statement (KFS) disclosing APR, processing fee, and total cost — before you sign.

Data Localisation

All payment and customer data is stored on servers located within India, in accordance with RBI circulars on data storage.

Consent-First

Granular, revocable consent through Account Aggregator (AA) rails. We never share your data without explicit permission.

Grievance Redressal

Two-tier grievance mechanism with a named Nodal Officer and escalation to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

Fair Practices Code

We follow the RBI Fair Practices Code for NBFCs — no coercive recovery, no hidden charges, no dark patterns.

Cyber Resilience

Aligned with the RBI Cyber Security Framework and CERT-In guidelines. Incidents are reported within prescribed timelines.
