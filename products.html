Products

Regulated products, transparently priced.

FinZ Personal Loan

Unsecured personal loans of ₹ 10,000 to ₹ 5,00,000, with APR disclosed upfront in the KFS. Tenure 3 to 24 months.

Regulated under: RBI NBFC Directions, RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.

FinZ Pay (UPI)

Built on the NPCI Unified Payments Interface. Zero MDR on P2P and small-value merchant payments, as per NPCI operating guidelines.

Regulated under: Payment & Settlement Systems Act, 2007; NPCI circulars.

FinZ Save

Sweep savings proposition through our regulated partner banks. Deposits are with the partner bank and eligible for DICGC cover up to ₹ 5 lakh.

In partnership with RBI-licensed scheduled commercial banks.

FinZ Invest (Mutual Funds)

Direct mutual fund investments through a SEBI-registered entity. Zero commissions. Complete disclosure of risks.

Regulated under: SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.

FinZ Credit Report

View your free credit report from RBI-accredited Credit Information Companies (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF), with your consent.

Regulated under: Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005.

FinZ for Business

Working capital lines for MSMEs with Account Aggregator-powered underwriting. No hidden charges, full KFS transparency.

Regulated under: RBI NBFC Directions.
