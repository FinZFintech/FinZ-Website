Interest Rate Policy

Board-approved. Last reviewed: 15 March 2026.

1. Background

This Policy has been framed in line with the RBI Master Direction on Scale Based Regulation for NBFCs, which require every NBFC to adopt an appropriate interest rate model and disclose the same on its website.

2. Risk-Based Pricing Framework

The rate of interest for each borrower is arrived at using a risk-based pricing model that considers:

  • Cost of funds for FinZ (weighted average borrowing cost)
  • Operating costs of underwriting and servicing the loan
  • Credit risk premium, derived from the borrower's credit bureau score, income, employment, stability, debt-to-income, and behavioural variables
  • Tenor premium
  • Margin / return on assets as approved by the Board

3. Range of Rates

Depending on the product and the borrower's risk profile, the annualised interest rate typically ranges between 14% and 32% per annum. The APR (inclusive of all fees and charges) is disclosed in the Key Fact Statement before loan execution.

4. Processing Fees

Processing fees range from 1% to 3% of the sanctioned amount plus applicable GST and are recovered upfront. All fees are transparently disclosed in the KFS.

5. Penal Charges (not Penal Interest)

In line with the RBI Circular on Fair Lending Practice — Penal Charges in Loan Accounts (2023), any charge on account of default or non-compliance of material terms is levied as a flat penal charge (not compounded interest) and is clearly disclosed in the loan agreement.

6. Foreclosure & Prepayment

No foreclosure or prepayment charges are levied on floating-rate retail loans to individual borrowers, in line with the RBI directive. For fixed-rate loans, if any, charges if levied will be disclosed in the KFS.

7. Communication of Changes

Any change in the rate of interest or charges applicable to existing loans will be communicated in advance and applied prospectively only.

8. Review

This Policy is reviewed by the Board of Directors at least once every financial year, or earlier if warranted by material changes in market conditions or regulation.
