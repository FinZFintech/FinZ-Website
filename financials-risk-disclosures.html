5. Risk management framework

The Board of Directors has approved a Risk Management Framework covering the principal risks to the Company:

  • Credit risk — governed by the Credit Policy, borrower due-diligence standards, bureau-based underwriting and portfolio-level concentration limits.
  • Operational risk — controlled through process standardisation, maker-checker controls, segregation of duties, and annual internal-audit review.
  • Liquidity & ALM risk — managed by the ALCO (Asset-Liability Committee) using structural liquidity statements, dynamic liquidity gaps, and stress testing per the RBI Liquidity Risk Management Framework.
  • Market risk — monitored for interest-rate sensitivity and refinancing risk on borrowings.
  • IT & cyber risk — governed by the Board-approved IT & Cyber Security Policy, aligned with the RBI Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (2023) and the CERT-In Directions, 2022.
  • Compliance & conduct risk — overseen by the Compliance function reporting directly to the Board, with periodic review of all regulatory filings and Fair Practices Code compliance.

7. Asset Liability Management (ALM) & Liquidity

Structural liquidity, dynamic liquidity and interest-rate sensitivity statements are reviewed by the ALCO on a quarterly basis in line with the RBI Liquidity Risk Management Framework for NBFCs. Summary disclosures, if required by the applicable SBR layer, are appended to the annual audited financial statements.

8. Related-party transactions & concentration

Related-party transactions, exposures to single / group borrowers, and concentration of advances are disclosed in the notes to the annual audited financial statements in the format prescribed by the RBI Master Direction on Disclosures.
