Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{PRIVACY_LAST_UPDATED}}. This Privacy Policy explains how FinZ Finance Private Limited ("FinZ", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, processes and shares your personal data when you interact with our website, mobile application, call centres or any other service channel. We handle your data in accordance with the Reserve Bank of India's guidelines (including the Master Direction on KYC, 2016 and the Digital Lending Guidelines, 2022), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who is the data fiduciary?

FinZ Finance Private Limited (CIN U64990UP2024PTC205686) is the data fiduciary responsible for personal data processed under this Policy. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at privacy@finz.finance (email preferred) or by post at the Registered Office below.

2. Categories of personal data collected

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, gender, PAN, Aadhaar reference number (masked), photograph, signature.
  • Contact data: residential address, email, mobile number.
  • Financial data: bank account number, IFSC, income, employer, salary slips, bank statements, credit bureau score and report.
  • KYC documents: DigiLocker XML, Aadhaar eKYC/OKYC, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Passport, utility bills where permitted by law.
  • Device & log data: IP address, device model, OS, browser, timestamp of access, referrer URL, geolocation (when granted).
  • Behavioural data: application journey steps, timing, and click paths — used for fraud prevention and UX improvement.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Evaluating and processing your loan application, including credit assessment and KYC verification.
  • Setting up and servicing loan accounts (disbursal, EMI, statements, closure).
  • Reporting to credit information companies (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark).
  • Prevention and detection of fraud, money-laundering, and cyber-crime.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory and audit obligations.
  • Communicating with you about your account, transactions, and service updates.
  • Marketing — only with your consent — for new products and offers.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent and/or our legitimate interests (credit risk, fraud prevention), and in performance of our contract with you, and to meet legal obligations as a regulated NBFC-ICC. For marketing, we rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Sharing of personal data

  • Lending partners & co-lenders (see Lending Partners) for credit decisioning and loan funding.
  • Credit Information Companies for reporting and obtaining credit reports.
  • KYC / verification service providers: Signzy, DigiLocker, NSDL, Account Aggregators (as onboarded) — all under written contracts that require compliance with this Policy and the DPDP Act.
  • Empanelled recovery agencies (see Collection Agencies) for overdue servicing — strictly on a need-to-know basis.
  • Statutory authorities, regulators, courts or law enforcement when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing.

6. Data localisation & security

As required by the RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines, all customer data is stored on servers physically located within India. We use TLS 1.3 for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for staff, and periodic VAPT assessments. Our incident response process complies with the CERT-In Directions of 2022.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data for the tenure of your relationship with us plus the statutory retention period — a minimum of 8 years from loan closure (per RBI/PMLA norms). Incomplete loan applications are purged automatically after 7 days of inactivity. Audit logs are retained per CERT-In guidance (180 days rolling).

8. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

  • Right to access a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Right to correction of inaccurate or misleading data and completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure of data (subject to legal and regulatory retention requirements).
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Right of grievance redressal (see Grievance Redressal).
  • Right to withdraw consent, where processing is based solely on consent.

To exercise any right, email privacy@finz.finance. We respond within the timelines prescribed by the DPDP Act.

9. Cookies & similar technologies

Our website uses essential cookies for session management and optional analytics cookies (only with your consent via the cookie banner). You can manage cookie preferences via your browser or the on-site settings. No cookie is used to serve third-party advertising.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. Where a student under 18 is a co-beneficiary of an education loan, the contractual relationship is only with the parent/guardian, whose data is processed under this Policy.

11. Updates to this Policy

We may amend this Policy from time to time. Material changes are notified to registered users via email or SMS. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
