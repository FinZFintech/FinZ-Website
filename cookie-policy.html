Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how it is used.

Categories of cookies we use

  • Strictly Necessary: Required for login, session, CSRF protection, and fraud prevention. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference: Remember language, theme, and accessibility settings.
  • Analytics (opt-in): First-party analytics to measure page performance and improve the experience. No data is shared with third-party ad networks.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent the Platform from functioning.

Do Not Track

We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal and "Do Not Track" headers for analytics cookies.
