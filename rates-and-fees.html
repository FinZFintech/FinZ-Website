Rates, Fees & Charges

Last reviewed by the Board on {{RATES_LAST_REVIEWED}}. FinZ Finance Private Limited's Interest Rate Policy is approved by the Board and reviewed at least annually. The rate applicable to each borrower depends on risk grade, tenure, product, loan amount and past repayment behaviour. The Annualised Percentage Rate (APR) — inclusive of all charges — is disclosed in the Key Fact Statement (KFS) issued before loan sanction.

Transparency commitment: No fee is ever deducted or charged without explicit mention in your KFS and loan agreement. If you find a discrepancy, raise a complaint via Grievance Redressal and we will respond within 15 business days.

How we determine your rate

Our risk-based pricing model considers:

  • Credit bureau score (CIBIL / Experian / Equifax) and repayment history
  • Debt-to-income (FOIR) and household indebtedness
  • Loan amount, tenure and end-use
  • Co-applicant strength for education loans
  • Employment stability and employer category for employee loans
  • Prevailing cost of funds and operating margin approved by the Board

Penal charges — important note

Per RBI circular on Fair Lending Practice — Penal Charges in Loan Accounts (DOR.MCS.REC.28/01.01.001/2023-24 dated 18 April 2023), penal charges levied for non-compliance with material terms and conditions of the loan contract are not capitalised (not added to principal) and no further interest is computed on such penal charges. Penal charges are reasonable and disclosed in the Key Fact Statement.

Interest calculation

  • Interest is computed on a reducing-balance basis on the outstanding principal.
  • EMIs are calculated using the standard amortisation formula and disclosed in your amortisation schedule.
  • Part-prepayment reduces principal; tenure or EMI is re-adjusted per loan agreement.

Key Fact Statement

Before you sign the loan agreement, you receive a standardised Key Fact Statement (KFS) in a format prescribed by RBI. The KFS shows the APR, all fees, cooling-off period (3 working days) and recovery mechanism. Any fee or charge not listed in the KFS is not payable by you.
