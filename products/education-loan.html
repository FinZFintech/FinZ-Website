Education Loan

Fund the skilling and schooling that shapes the future.

Education loans from FinZ Finance support tuition, coaching, examination and other allied costs for students enrolled in recognised Indian schools, boards and skill-development / certification programmes. For undergraduate, postgraduate or study-abroad financing, please see our Higher Education Loan. Repayment options flex with academic calendars and co-applicant income.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Student (borrower or co-borrower)

  • Indian citizen or resident
  • Enrolled in a recognised school or skill-development / certification programme
  • Age 18+ (or with parent/guardian as primary borrower)
  • Valid PAN & Aadhaar (for co-borrower or adult student)

Parent / Guardian co-applicant

  • Salaried or self-employed with verifiable income
  • Minimum income as per internal credit policy
  • Bureau score, debt-to-income and FOIR per internal credit policy
  • Bank account for EMI debit via eNACH
Please note: Final eligibility is determined post credit assessment. Disbursal is at FinZ Finance's sole discretion per the approved Credit Policy.
Documents

What you'll need

Identity & address

  • PAN card (borrower + co-borrower)
  • Aadhaar (for eKYC via DigiLocker / XML)
  • Recent passport-size photograph

Academic & financial

  • Admission letter / fee schedule from institution
  • Last 3 months salary slips / 6 months bank statements
  • Form 16 or ITR (for self-employed co-applicant)
  • Bank account details for eNACH mandate

Responsible recovery

FinZ Finance follows the Fair Practices Code approved by its Board.
