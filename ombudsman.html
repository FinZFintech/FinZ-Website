RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021

One Nation, One Ombudsman — for banks, NBFCs, payment system participants and more.

The Reserve Bank of India — Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 provides a cost-free alternate grievance resolution mechanism for customers of regulated entities, including NBFCs like FinZ Fintech Private Limited.

When can I approach the Ombudsman?

  • You have filed a written complaint with FinZ and we have rejected the complaint; or
  • The reply received is not satisfactory; or
  • No reply has been received within 30 days of lodging the complaint with FinZ.

The complaint must be filed within one year from the date of our reply (or one year and 30 days from lodging the complaint if no reply).

How to file

  1. Online (recommended): Visit https://cms.rbi.org.in and file a complaint under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
  2. Phone: Call the RBI's toll-free number 14448 (9:30 AM to 5:15 PM, working days) and a contact-centre executive can help you lodge the complaint in your preferred language.
  3. Email / Post: Email CRPC@rbi.org.in or write to: Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre, Reserve Bank of India, 4th Floor, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017.

What the Ombudsman can decide

The Ombudsman can award compensation up to ₹ 20 lakh for actual loss suffered by the complainant as a direct consequence of the act or omission of the regulated entity, plus an additional amount up to ₹ 1 lakh for loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish.

Display of Information

As required under Clause 15 of the Scheme, the salient features of the Scheme in English, Hindi, and Marathi, along with the contact details of our Principal Nodal Officer, are prominently displayed at our registered office and all branches.
