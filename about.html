About Us

Responsible lending, built for a digital India.

FinZ Finance Private Limited is an RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company – Investment and Credit Company (NBFC-ICC). We combine modern technology with the discipline of a regulated lender to offer transparent, compliant and customer-friendly credit products.

Our mission

To make formal credit accessible, transparent and respectful for every eligible Indian — starting with education financing and employee liquidity solutions, and expanding to adjacent retail credit segments over time.

What we stand for

  • Transparent pricing. Every borrower receives a Key Fact Statement (KFS) disclosing the Annualised Percentage Rate (APR), processing fees, insurance charges and recovery charges before signing the loan agreement.
  • Responsible recovery. Collections are governed by our Fair Practices Code and the RBI's Code of Conduct. We do not tolerate coercive or harassing behaviour from any staff member or empanelled agency.
  • Data privacy by design. Customer data is stored and processed only within India in line with the RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Responsible credit access. As an NBFC-ICC under the RBI's Scale-Based Regulation framework, we extend credit on the basis of verifiable income, bureau assessment and internal credit policy — with appropriate risk-based pricing and no hidden charges.
