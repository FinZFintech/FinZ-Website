About FinZ

Building regulated, responsible, and delightful digital finance for India.

Who we are

FinZ Fintech Private Limited is a non-banking financial company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, and registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). We design digital-first products across lending, payments, and wealth, built on a foundation of customer protection and regulatory integrity.

Our mission

To make formal finance accessible to the next 500 million Indians — in a way that is transparent, affordable, and compliant with the letter and spirit of the RBI's supervisory framework.

Our principles

Consumer-first

No dark patterns. Prices disclosed in the KFS. Easy exit within the cooling-off period.

Compliance by design

Regulatory requirements are built into our product flows, not bolted on afterwards.

Security & resilience

ISO/IEC 27001 aligned, aligned with the RBI Cyber Security Framework, regularly stress-tested.

Data stewardship

We collect the minimum necessary data, for specified purposes, with revocable consent.

Leadership & governance

The company is overseen by a Board of Directors that includes independent directors with experience in banking, technology, and consumer protection. Our committees cover Audit, Risk, Nomination & Remuneration, and Customer Service, in line with RBI governance requirements for Middle/Upper Layer NBFCs (where applicable).
