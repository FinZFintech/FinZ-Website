Grievance Redressal

Three-tier escalation, as prescribed by the RBI Fair Practices Code and Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

Turnaround times: Level 1 — acknowledgement within 24 hours, resolution within 7 working days. Level 2 — resolution within 15 working days. Level 3 — escalate to RBI Ombudsman if unresolved within 30 days.

Level 1 — Customer Care

Please reach out to our customer care team first.

  • Email: care@finzfintech.in
  • Helpline: 1800-000-0000 (toll-free, Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST)
  • Write to us: Customer Care, FinZ Fintech Pvt. Ltd., Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051

Level 3 — RBI Integrated Ombudsman

If your complaint is not resolved within 30 days of lodging with us, or you are dissatisfied with the resolution, you may approach the Reserve Bank of India — Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

  • Complaint portal: https://cms.rbi.org.in
  • Toll-free: 14448 (9:30 AM to 5:15 PM, working days)
  • Email: CRPC@rbi.org.in
  • Post: Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre, Reserve Bank of India, 4th Floor, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017

What to include in your complaint

  1. Your full name and registered mobile/email
  2. Loan/transaction reference number (if any)
  3. Description of the issue with dates
  4. Copies of relevant communication or screenshots
  5. Your preferred resolution

Complaints regarding Digital Lending Apps (DLAs)

Complaints specifically related to FinZ's digital lending app(s) may also be lodged on the RBI CMS portal. Our in-app "Help & Support" section provides one-click complaint lodging with auto-generated reference numbers, as required under the RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.

Accessibility

Grievance services are available in English and Hindi. Regional language support is available on request for Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and Gujarati.
